Hi, I’m Qingwan (I also go by Cecelia)! I’m currently a **Data Analyst at Dell Technologies**, where I transform manual reporting process into an automated analytics experience using Python, SQL, PowerBI, and Jira. My work visualizes team and product performance to inform leadership decisions. I also support patent work and LLM training efforts that help engineers troubleshoot more efficiently.

After work, I am an **educator** and **researcher**. As the **Assistant Director** of the Aftermath Learning Lab, I mentor undergraduate research assistants in writing academic papers, applying for grants, and organizing conferences. I integrate generative models like ChatGPT into playful learning and participatory design, creating design-thinking workshops where students develop educational board games on textile waste. I scaled the workshops to 200+ college and high school students, enabling them to reflect on responsible AI use and textile sustainability through hands-on, creative engagement.

I am also deeply committed to **community service**. At Dell, I lead the **GenNext NE Grad Programming Pillar**, helping colleagues navigate pathways to graduate school and professional certifications. Beyond Dell, I serve as the **IEEE Boston Section Young Professionals Vice Chair**, organizing events that bring young engineers across Boston together to meet, learn, and connect.

I graduated from **Boston College** in 2023 with a B.A. in **Applied Psychology** and **Computer Science**, along with a minor in Cyberstrategy and Design. I was honored to receive the **General Excellence Award** for my academic and community contributions.
