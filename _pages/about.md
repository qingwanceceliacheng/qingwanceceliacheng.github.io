Hi, I’m Qingwan (I also go by Cecelia)! I’m currently a Data Analyst at Dell Technologies, where I specialize in automating reporting and transforming data into meaningful insights using tools like Power BI and Jira. One of my favorite projects involved redesigning a manual reporting process into a fully automated, 20+ page dashboard. By integrating Jira APIs, mapping complex datasets, defining key KPIs, and designing user-informed visualizations, I helped our team track performance, identify trends, and make smarter decisions with confidence. I also supported agile engineering teams by optimizing sprint tracking, visualizing ticket flows, and streamlining administrative workflows. Outside of my analyst role, I co-lead several Employee Resource Groups—organizing cultural events and hosting sessions to support colleagues navigating the path to graduate school. I’m passionate about creating spaces for growth, inclusion, and shared learning—both in and beyond the workplace.

After work, I serve as the Program Manager at Aftermath Learning Lab, where I lead a team of undergraduate researchers in writing academic papers, applying for grants, and organizing conferences. I also design and facilitate workshops that integrate design thinking and generative models with educational game development to promote sustainability and systems thinking. So far, I’ve developed engaging learning experiences for over 100 college and high school students. I’m especially passionate about using design thinking and technology to create meaningful, playful educational tools or experiences that empower young people to imagine and build a better future. 

I graduated from Boston College in 2023 with a B.A. in Applied Psychology and Computer Science, along with a minor in Cyberstrategy and Design. I was honored to receive the General Excellence Award for my academic and community contributions.
