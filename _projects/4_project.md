**How might we create joyful, place-based opportunities for people across generations to improve planetary health?**

Community Crates is a pop-up recreational space designed to bring together people of all ages in shared activities that strengthen relationships, foster local environmental awareness, and encourage collective action. The goal is to make environmental engagement feel fun, inclusive, and rooted in the everyday lives of community members.

We began with a wide-ranging survey and interviews across age groups and geographic locations—from elementary school students to retirees, from rural communities to urban neighborhoods. The results were clear:
- 93% of participants expressed interest in helping to improve their community
- 87.5% believed it’s important for young and older people to talk to each other about community issues

To further understand community needs, we created personas—an elementary school student, a single data analyst, a working mom, and a retired grandmother. A common theme emerged: everyone regularly visits big-box retail stores. These stores became our opportunity space—specifically, their underutilized parking lots.

We chose to transform a corner of these parking lots into vibrant hubs for social and environmental interaction. Each Community Crate installation is made from recycled materials and reflects values of inclusion, creativity, and public life. The modular design allows the crates to travel between cities, offering flexible, repeatable impact. A crate delivery truck drops off the units, and nearby residents can use a mobile app to collaboratively build the space. The app guides them through the process: it shows how many people are needed, how much time it will take, and how to assemble each part—turning setup into a community-building experience in itself.

Once assembled, the space comes alive. Features include:
- A **performance stage** for music, dance, or storytelling
- A **playscape** for children and families
- An **exercise node** for community yoga or Zumba
- A **vendor area** for food, drinks, and local businesses

Together, these elements create a space that’s not only joyful and intergenerational, but also socially, economically, and environmentally impactful. Community Crates reimagines public space as a catalyst for planetary health—one parking lot at a time.
